## Highlights

### Blaster: the silent `admit` is gone

@felipeperet closed out two of the reconstruction issues he filed in W34:
reconstruction failure now surfaces a diagnostic instead of a silent `admit`
([#211](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/211), closing
[#171](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/171)), and the
Bool↔Prop bridge on And/Or emits proof steps
([#210](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/210), closing
[#174](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/174)). Behind them,
eight further issues map what remains of the module's proof coverage — the
decide-of-equality and `DecideBool`/`DecideProp` reductions, the Bool-not/decide
bridge, constructor and boolean-constant equality, a reconstruction-module audit
and profiling of the proof stack replay
([#212](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/212)–[#219](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/219))
— with two PRs already open against them
([#220](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/220) from
@MarcoNardell1,
[#221](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/221)). A whole
subsystem is being mapped and worked by contributors outside the team; thank you.

From the team, `abbrev` resolution in co-domain evaluation merged
([#193](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/193)) and three
representation tracks were filed — BitVector and ByteArray opacifications
([#207](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/207)), migrating
String representations to bytestrings
([#208](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/208)) and a Lean 4
toolchain bump
([#209](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/209)) — while the
GHC plug-in spike closed
([#156](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/156)). In
PlutusCoreBlaster, @geofflittle opened fuel-free step iteration and composition
lemmas for the CEK machine
([#42](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/42)), and his
two `Data`-codec threads from W30 closed
([#22](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/22),
[#24](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/24)).
