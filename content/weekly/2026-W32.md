## Highlights

### Blaster: an outside conformance review of the UPLC formalization

The week's largest single body of work came from @jmchapman, who went through
PlutusCoreBlaster and CardanoLedgerApiBlaster against the Haskell reference and
filed what he found: BLS12-381 memory-size cost constants using raw byte counts
instead of the word-based convention
([#31](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/31)),
Case-on-constants not gated behind a protocol version unlike upstream
([#32](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/32)),
`ConstrData` not enforcing the Word64 index range
([#33](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/33)), a stale
conformance suite making BLS12-381 tests fail budget checks by 70–105x
([#35](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/35)), and a
missing `MkCons` type-tag check
([#37](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/37)) — with
three PRs opened alongside them
([#34](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/34),
[#36](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/36),
[#38](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/38)) — and, in
the ledger API, `validOutputs` and `validReferenceInput` rejecting contexts the
ledger permits ([#8](https://github.com/input-output-hk/CardanoLedgerApiBlaster/issues/8)), with a PR removing datum conditions the
ledger does not enforce ([#9](https://github.com/input-output-hk/CardanoLedgerApiBlaster/pull/9)). That is
exactly the scrutiny a formalization needs, and we're grateful for it.

More from outside the team the same week: @colll78 opened fixes making `valueOf`
SMT-translatable
([#7](https://github.com/input-output-hk/CardanoLedgerApiBlaster/pull/7)) and
correcting `ScriptPurpose`/`Credential` orderings
([#6](https://github.com/input-output-hk/CardanoLedgerApiBlaster/pull/6)), plus
rewrite-cache reuse in the optimizer
([#160](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/160)); @paolino
reported a `Blake2b_256` decode with no CEK evaluator dispatch
([#28](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/28)) and
opened a cross-project channel for Blaster on Aiken on-chain verification
([#161](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/161)), both closed the same week; and
@felipeperet found constant folding of `Int.tdiv`/`tmod`/`fdiv`/`fmod` using
`ediv`/`emod` semantics, wrong on negative operands
([#162](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/162)).

From the team: performance optimizations opened in Lean-blaster
([#155](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/155)), and a BLS
axiomatization ([#29](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/29))
and HOF-free `encodeData` formalization
([#30](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/30)) in
PlutusCoreBlaster. Four tracks were filed for scheduling: a Z3 backend

([#159](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/159)), a GHC
