## Highlights

### Blaster: proof reconstruction gets a work plan

@felipeperet filed four issues covering the reconstruction module: emit proof
steps for `optimizeIff` and the And/Or implies-reductions
([#172](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/172)), for the
hypothesis-driven And/Or reductions and reorder
([#173](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/173)), and for the
Bool↔Prop bridge
([#174](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/174)) — plus
replacing the silent `admit` on reconstruction failure
([#171](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/171)), which is
the difference between a failed replay being visible and being silent. The team
filed the crypto axiomatization work in three parts — hash functions
([#166](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/166)), signature
checks ([#167](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/167)) and
further implementations
([#168](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/168)) — alongside
Blaster optimizations
([#169](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/169)) and a Plinth
bridge ([#170](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/170)).
PlutusCoreBlaster's README expansion, with docs, a table of contents and badges,
went up for review
([#39](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/39)).
