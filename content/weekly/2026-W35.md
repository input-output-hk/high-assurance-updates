## Highlights

### Blaster: a soundness audit of the SMT encoding

Four findings were filed against the encoding itself: qualifier-guarded
extensionality plus unguarded lambda-definition axioms makes the SMT theory
inconsistent, so Blaster admits `False`
([#194](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/194)); `isFun` is
unsatisfiable for lambdas over qualified codomain arrows
([#195](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/195)); argument
qualifiers are emitted unguarded at the `cstr` and `co_cstr` sites
([#196](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/196)); and
`Undetermined` goldens are not soundness evidence
([#197](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/197)). Two PRs
guarding quantifiers with domain qualifiers are open against the first two
([#198](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/198),
[#199](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/199)). A tool that
can prove `False` proves nothing; these are the right bugs to be loud about.

### Blaster: six proof-reconstruction PRs, all from outside the team

@felipeperet and @MarcoNardell1 turned last week's reconstruction plan into
merged code: proof steps for `optimizeIff` and the And/Or implies-reductions
([#200](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/200)), the
hypothesis-driven And/Or reductions
([#206](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/206)) and
`optimizeBoolAnd`/`optimizeBoolOr`
([#203](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/203)); Bool/Prop
negation equality hardening
([#205](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/205)); a fix for
`andBoolReduction?` returning `Prop.False` rather than `Bool.false`
([#177](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/177)); and a
proof-replay mistelescoping fix, reported and closed the same week
([#202](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/202),
[#201](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/201)). Four further
Eq-hardening issues were filed behind them
([#178](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/178)–[#181](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/181)).
Sustained, careful work — thank you both.

Elsewhere: string literal translation to SMT-LIB merged
([#175](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/175)), with `abbrev`
resolution ([#193](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/pull/193)) and
