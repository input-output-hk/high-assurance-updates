## Highlights

### Blaster: `serialiseData` hit its limits, and the reports came from users

Three bugs filed from outside the team all point at the same seam — encoding
`Data` under symbolic execution. @Luivatra reported that Blaster never
terminates translating structural recursion through a `List.foldlM` closure in
`encodeData` ([#152](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/152))
and that `BitVec`'s instance parameters are unsupported at the UInt8-level byte
encoding in `encodeBytestring`
([#153](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/153),
[#26](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/issues/26)), then
opened a PR making the `SerializeData` builtin's encoder opaque to symbolic
tools ([#27](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/27)).
@colll78 reported separately that the optimizer emits kernel-ill-typed
`Blaster.dite'` while the test harness never typechecks, so the expectations
encode the bad terms
([#154](https://github.com/input-output-hk/Lean-blaster/issues/154)). Detailed,
reproducible reports from real usage — thank you both. @geofflittle's
indefinite-length CBOR decode support also merged
([#25](https://github.com/input-output-hk/PlutusCoreBlaster/pull/25)).

### Property-based testing

`TestingInterface` slot handling was fixed and the use cases updated to match
([#84](https://github.com/input-output-hk/sc-testing-tools/pull/84)), and the
JSON schema's nullability change merged
([#78](https://github.com/input-output-hk/sc-testing-tools/pull/78)).

and state views
([#18](https://github.com/input-output-hk/sc-testing-tools-vs-extension/pull/18)).
